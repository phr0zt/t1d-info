Kit that has to survive the session

Where the sensor goes

At least an inch from an infusion site. Away from impact zones in contact sport, and off the lower back for anyone rolling or tumbling.

Keeping it stuck on

Sweat, heat and water all beat adhesive. Clean and fully dry the skin, then a barrier wipe or an overlay patch.

False lows from pressure

Tight compression kit, or lying on the sensor, can produce readings that are simply wrong. Odd overnight dips after training are often this.

Taking the pump off

Fine for swimming, water sport or contact sport — but past about 90 minutes you need a plan to replace the missed insulin, or you come back high with ketones climbing.

The named hazard in young children is simply forgetting to reconnect afterwards. Set a phone alarm. Sources: Riddell 2017 · ISPAD 2022 · ADA 2016.

Some competitions prohibit wearing devices. Worth checking rules in advance rather than at the start line.
