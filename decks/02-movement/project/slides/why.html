Most people with type 1 do no structured exercise at all

Around 60% do none, and fewer than one in five manage aerobic exercise more than twice a week. The reasons are consistent across every study that asks: fear of going low, fear of losing control of the numbers, and simply not having been taught how.

In children, guidelines name parental hesitancy outright as a barrier. That is a documented obstacle, not a personal failing.

This deck is about removing the third reason, so the first two stop deciding.

Sources: Riddell et al. 2017 exercise consensus · ISPAD 2022 · T1DEXI

Naming parental fear openly tends to land well. Parents rarely hear it described as a known, expected obstacle rather than overprotectiveness.
