How much, according to Diabetes Canada

150 minutes a week

Moderate to vigorous aerobic activity, spread over at least three days, with no more than two days in a row off.

90 to 140 minutes still helps, just less.

Resistance, twice a week

Three is better, and in addition to the aerobic work, not instead of it. Start with one set of 15 to 20 reps at a moderate weight and build.

Break up sitting

Get up briefly every 20 to 30 minutes. This is a separate recommendation from the 150 minutes, and it is easier to start with.

Children: 60 minutes a day

Mostly aerobic, with vigorous and muscle- and bone-strengthening activity on at least three days a week.

Only about 30% of adults with type 1 reach 150 minutes. If you are starting from nothing, the gap between zero and something is worth more than the gap between 100 and 150.

The two-consecutive-days rule is the one people miss. It exists because the insulin-sensitivity benefit fades within roughly 48 hours.
