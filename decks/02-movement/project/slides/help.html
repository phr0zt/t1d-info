Starting, and where to get help

Start smaller than you think

A twenty-minute walk, at the same time of day, three times a week. Same conditions means readable patterns.

Write it down

Activity, time of day, what you ate, what insulin was on board, what happened after. Three sessions of notes beats any general rule.
