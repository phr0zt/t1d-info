Same name, different candy

Popular US charts don’t travel well. Four things to know before you copy one into a Canadian kitchen.

Smarties are chocolate here

Canadian Smarties are candy-coated chocolate, slowed by fat. The US dextrose candy is sold in Canada as Rockets.

Pieces aren’t pieces

The US chart says 5 Sour Patch Kids = 15 g. On the Canadian bag, 5 is about 11 g, so you need 7.

Some rows don’t exist here

Gushers, Brach’s, some Welch’s pouches, candy corn: US pack sizes. Use the label on what you can actually buy.

Chews have fat

Skittles and Starburst contain palm kernel oil. They work, just a little slower than jelly beans or Rockets.

Sources: smarties.com (“In Canada, our Smarties candy is known as Rockets”) · Mondelez Canada label · Mars US labels · Beyond Type 1 chart, 2026, whose own text and image disagree on Sour Patch Kids.

The Smarties mix-up is the one that matters. A parent who read a US list buys the Canadian box and treats a low with chocolate.
