Where to keep it

On you

A pocket, a purse, the phone case. A roll of Rockets or 4 tablets weighs almost nothing.

Bed, car, gym bag

Night lows and driving lows are the ones you can’t walk to the kitchen for. In a hot car gummies melt; tablets cope better.

School, daycare, work

A labelled kit with the amount written on it, so anyone can hand it over without asking.

Check it monthly

Restock what got eaten, and check expiry dates. Tablets crumble in damp; juice boxes burst when they freeze.

Practical guidance; no study compares storage spots. Your diabetes team or school nurse can help with a school plan.

The best treatment is the one within reach. The fastest sugar in a cupboard across town does nothing.
