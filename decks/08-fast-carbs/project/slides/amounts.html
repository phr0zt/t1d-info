Not everyone needs 15 g

Children: by age

Under 5: 5 g (1 tablet, 40 mL juice)

5 to 10: 10 g (2–3 tablets, 85 mL)

Over 10: 15 g (4 tablets, 125 mL)

Needs help, can swallow

20 g for an adult: 5 Dex4, 200 mL (¾ cup) pop or juice, or 3 rolls of Rockets. Can’t swallow: glucagon and 911.

Automated pumps

If the pump already paused insulin, 15 g can overshoot. ISPAD suggests considering 5 to 10 g. That’s expert opinion, so ask your team.

Many need more

A Canadian study found people used an average of 32 g. Start at the standard amount, recheck, and set your own with your team.

Sources: Diabetes Canada 2018, ch. 34, table 2 (children) and ch. 14 (20 g) · Diabète Québec · ISPAD 2022, ch. 12 (AID: grade E, expert opinion) · EASD/ADA AID consensus 2022.

This is where the bodies disagree, so say so. Diabetes Canada’s 2018 guideline predates automated pumps; ISPAD and the international AID consensus say use less. ISPAD doses children by weight (0.3 g/kg) instead of age.
