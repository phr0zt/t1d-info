Labels: Rockets and Sour Patch Kids, Canadian; Skittles, Starburst, Life Savers and Jelly Belly, US. Recipes and piece sizes change, so the label on your bag wins.

Rockets are pressed dextrose: the same sugar as glucose tablets, in a roll that fits a pocket. Diabète Québec lists 2 rolls. Diabetes Canada says 6 Life Savers at 2.5 g each; the current US label works out to about 3.75 g each.
