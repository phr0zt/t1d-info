The 15-15 rule

Under 4.0 mmol/L (3.9 on many meters and CGM reports), Diabetes Canada and Diabète Québec give the same four steps.

15 g

Take it

Fast-acting carbohydrate, ideally glucose or sugar as tablets or dissolved in water.

15 min

Sit and wait

Don’t keep eating while you wait. It takes time to work.

Check again

Still under 4.0? Take another 15 g and wait again.

Then eat

The meal that was due, or, if it’s more than an hour away, a snack with 15 g of carbohydrate and some protein.

Sources: Diabetes Canada 2018 CPG, ch. 14 · Diabète Québec, « L’hypoglycémie : comprendre et traiter ». These are adult amounts; children are on slide 9.

Diabetes Canada’s 2018 guideline uses under 4.0; Diabète Québec and most CGM reports use under 3.9. Same advice either way.
