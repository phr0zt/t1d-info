Treat it like medicine

A low makes you ravenous. That’s biology, not weak willpower. Overtreating is the most common mistake, and it’s understandable.

Pick a “medicine” candy

Something you don’t snack on for fun. Measured, boring, always the same amount.

Pre-portion it

Count 15 g into small bags or tins ahead of time. Then you can’t take a handful while you’re low.

Trust the clock

Your CGM can lag a meter by 5 to 15 minutes. Before treating a second time, confirm with a finger prick.

Then let it go

A rebound high after a low happens. It’s a number to correct, not a failure.

Sources: Beyond Type 1 (the “medicine, not food” idea) · ADA Safe at School CGM guidance 2023 · ISPAD 2022 · Diabète Québec.

The “medicine” framing takes the shame out of it. You aren’t eating candy; you’re taking a measured dose.
