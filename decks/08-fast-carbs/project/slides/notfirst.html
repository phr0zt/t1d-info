Not the first thing to grab

Chocolate

Including Canadian Smarties, chocolate bars, cookies and cake. Fat slows the sugar.

Milk and ice cream

Fat and protein. Diabetes Canada lists milk as slower.

Diet or zero drinks

No sugar at all. Keep the regular cans clearly apart from the diet ones.

Glucose gel, fruit, cereal

Gel: under 1.0 mmol/L at 20 minutes. Whole fruit and cereal are slow too.

None of these is dangerous. They just leave you low for longer, and they make it easy to eat far more than 15 g while you wait.

Sources: Diabetes Canada 2018, ch. 14 · ISPAD 2022, ch. 12 (avoid fat-containing foods such as chocolate as initial treatment).

If chocolate is the only thing there, eat it. Slow sugar beats no sugar. Then restock the real kit.
