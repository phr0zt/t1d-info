Make your own row

Any candy you like can join the chart. All you need is the Nutrition Facts table on the package.

36 g

1 · Read Carbohydrate

Find the serving (say, 16 pieces) and the Carbohydrate line (36 g). Use the total, not the Sugars line.

2.25 g

2 · Divide

36 g ÷ 16 pieces = 2.25 g per piece.

7

3 · Count to 15

15 ÷ 2.25 = 6.7. Round up: 7 pieces. Write it on the bag.

Pick candies with 0 g fat on the label: hard candy, jelly beans, gummies, Rockets. The fat line tells you whether it’s fast.

Writing the number on the bag in marker is the whole trick. At 3.1 mmol/L nobody does division.
