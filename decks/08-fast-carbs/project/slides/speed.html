Why some sugar works faster

+2.1

mmol/L within 20 minutes: what 15 g of glucose does, on average.

20 g of glucose: about +3.6 mmol/L at 45 minutes. Glucose gel: under 1.0 at 20 minutes.

Glucose: fastest

Glucose tablets and Rockets candy are pure glucose (dextrose), which needs no digesting.

Table sugar: close behind

Half glucose. Sugar dissolved in water and regular pop work well.

Fructose: slower

Fruit juice and honey carry a lot of fructose, which the body has to convert first.

Fat and protein: slowest

They hold sugar in the stomach. That rules out chocolate, milk and ice cream.

Sources: Diabetes Canada 2018 CPG, ch. 14 (glucose or sucrose tablets or solution are preferable to orange juice and glucose gels) · ISPAD 2022, ch. 12 (fructose-based treatment less effective).

Juice still works and is fine when it’s what’s there. It’s just not the fastest thing in the fridge. Regular pop beats juice.
