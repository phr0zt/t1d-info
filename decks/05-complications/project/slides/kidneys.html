Kidneys

58%

of people with early protein leakage saw it go back to normal over six years, in a long-running Boston study.

This is the one complication on the list that genuinely reverses in its early stage. That is worth knowing before anything else about it.

It moves slowly, in stages

Usually five years or more to pass through each stage. Slow means there is time to act — if someone is looking.

Early changes are not rare

Around 15% show some protein leakage by 15 years, 30% by 25 years. That is a marker to act on, not a diagnosis of kidney failure.

Kidney failure is the rare end

Roughly 3 to 4% at 25 to 30 years in recent population studies. Dialysis is not the expected destination.

What makes it regress

Blood pressure at target, glucose in range, lower cholesterol, and catching it early. Most of that is medication, not willpower.

Sources: Diabetes Canada CPG Ch.29 · Perkins/Joslin regression data · Frontiers in Nephrology 2023
