Book one appointment

Eyes, kidneys or feet — whichever is overdue. That single action is worth more than everything else on this slide.

Ask about the other four levers

Blood pressure, cholesterol, kidney protection, smoking. Most people with type 1 have never been walked through them.

Say it if it’s heavy

Distress and burnout are treatable and routinely go unmentioned. Bring it up the way you would a blood result.
