What that trial cost, and why your version is different

The honest price in 1993

Intensive management came with two to three times the rate of severe hypoglycemia. That was the real trade-off, and it was a serious one.

Anyone who tells you the DCCT was a free win has not read it.

Why that bargain has changed

Those people had no continuous glucose monitors, no alarms, no modern insulin analogues and no automated delivery.

Lower glucose and fewer severe lows are no longer opposing goals in the way they were then. The tools you have were built specifically to break that trade-off.

Source: DCCT 1993. Every risk figure in this deck comes from people managing type 1 with the technology of decades ago.

Stating the downside of the landmark trial openly is what makes the rest of the numbers believable.
