The rates have already fallen, hard

This is the most under-told part of the whole subject. Before the next section names what can go wrong, here is what has been happening to those numbers.

−77%

advanced eye disease

annual incidence in type 1, 1980 to 2007. Vision impairment fell 57% over the same period.

21%

eye disease at 15 years

in a Belgian national registry in 2022 — down from 36% in 2001.

3–4%

kidney failure at 25 to 30 years

across recent Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish and Japanese population studies. Low single digits, not the figure people imagine.

The honest caveat

Early markers are falling faster than hard endpoints, and one Swedish registry found no clear drop in kidney failure since 2001. The direction is good; it is not uniform.

Sources: NIH/NCBI diabetes in America · Belgian IQED registry · Frontiers in Nephrology 2023 · Swedish National Diabetes Register
