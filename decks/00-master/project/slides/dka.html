Ketoacidosis is the one that takes young lives

Of the years of life lost before fifty in Scotland’s national type 1 data, the largest single share came from DKA and diabetic coma — not from eyes, kidneys or nerves.

It is acute, fast, and preventable. Delay is what makes it dangerous.

Which makes sick-day rules and ketone strips the part of diabetes education that saves the most life-years — and the part most often skipped.

Go in now if

  • Ketones are moderate or large
  • Vomiting has gone on more than a couple of hours
  • Nothing will stay down
  • Glucose stays high after corrections
  • Breathing is fast and deep, or breath smells sweet
  • They are drowsy or hard to rouse

Nobody has ever been in trouble for arriving at an emergency department with ketones. Sources: Diabetes Canada CPG Ch.15 · Livingstone et al., Scotland.

Most patient material gives DKA a footnote and retinopathy a chapter. The mortality data say to do the reverse for a young audience.
