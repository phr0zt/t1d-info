How this deck is ordered, and why

First

The emergencies

A low and ketoacidosis. These come before everything because they are the parts that can hurt you this week.

Then

The daily three

Food, insulin timing and movement. The levers you pull several times a day, every day.

Then

The interference

Medications, alcohol, recreational drugs, illness and stress — everything else that moves the number.

Then

The long game

What the daily work is actually buying, what the targets are, and which appointments to book.

Last

The people

What family, friends and coworkers need to know — and what not to say.

Newly diagnosed? The first nine slides are the ones to read today. The rest will still be here next week.

Giving people permission to stop after the emergency section is deliberate. Newly diagnosed people are usually overwhelmed, and safety is the only part that cannot wait.
