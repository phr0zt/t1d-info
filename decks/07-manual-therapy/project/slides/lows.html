Lows on the table

Lying still, relaxed, eyes closed, face in a cradle: the early signs of a low are easy to miss — or to mistake for relaxation.

Before

Check. If you’re low or heading down, treat it and wait, or move the appointment. Eat as usual — don’t arrive on an empty stomach.

Within reach

Fast sugar on the table edge or in your hand, not in a bag across the room. Agree on one word that means “stop, I need sugar”.

After

Check again before you drive. Diabète Québec warns of unexpected drops; the FQM suggests checking before and after at least the first two or three sessions.

If it happens

Stop. Sit up slowly. 15 g of fast sugar, recheck in 15 minutes. Unconscious or seizing: 911, nothing by mouth.

Sources: Diabète Québec, « Spa, sauna et massothérapie » · FQM, « Masser une personne diabétique » · Diabetes Canada. No study has looked at lows during massage directly: this is precaution, not data.

The one word matters. Many people with type 1 won’t interrupt a session they are paying for. Give them permission in advance.
