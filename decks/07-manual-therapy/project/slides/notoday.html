When to move the appointment

Ketones raised

Blood ketones 0.6 or above, or high and unwell. That’s your sick-day plan, not a massage.

A severe low in the last 24 hours

One that needed someone else’s help. Your defences are blunted for the next one.

Wounds or infection

An open sore, ulcer, blister or infection in the area to be treated — especially on the feet.

Fever or acute illness

Sick days come first. Book again when you’re back to your usual numbers.

Everything else — newly diagnosed, pump or injections, existing complications — is a reason to tell the therapist, not a reason to stay home.

Sources: FQM 2018 · Diabète Québec 2014. Postponing for ketones or a recent severe low is clinical common sense rather than a published massage rule.

The draft advice circulating online calls for a doctor’s clearance before every booking. The FQM asks for one only when in doubt. Don’t medicalise a massage.
