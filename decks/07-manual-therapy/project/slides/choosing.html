Choosing a therapist in Québec

Check membership

The title isn’t protected, so the association is the check. FQM, Mon Réseau Plus and RITMA all publish member directories. Complaints go to the association.

Insurance receipts

Group plans usually require a member in good standing of an association your insurer recognises; some want a doctor’s recommendation. Check your insurer’s list before booking.

Public coverage and taxes

RAMQ doesn’t cover massage or orthotherapy. In Québec they don’t count for the federal medical expense credit, and generally not the provincial one. Physiotherapy does.

One question to ask

“Have you worked with clients who use insulin, a pump or a sensor?” A good answer sounds a lot like the previous slide.

Sources: FQM · Mon Réseau Plus (RMPQ) · RITMA · Desjardins group insurance claims guide · Canada Revenue Agency, authorized medical practitioners list (January 2026).

We don’t recommend specific clinics or associations. The point is that, in Québec, the patient does the checking that an order would do elsewhere.
