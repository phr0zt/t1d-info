What to tell the therapist

Before you lie down

  • I have type 1 diabetes and use insulin — a pump / injections.
  • My sensor is here, my pump site is here. No oil, pressure or stretching on them.
  • My last insulin was at ___, in my ___. Please skip that area, and no heat there.
  • My glucose right now is ___ and it’s steady / rising / falling.
  • If I say “sugar”, stop — I may be going low.
  • Numb areas: ___. Broad pressure, no heat. Also: stiff joints, wounds, dizziness standing up.

Bring

Your meter or phone, and fast sugar within reach of the table.

The printable card version of this slide is in the downloads. Hand it over at the first visit.

Built from the FQM intake questions for clients with diabetes and the sensor makers’ instructions.

Practise saying this once at home. People skip it at the clinic because it feels like a lot to explain to a stranger. The card does the explaining.
