Stiff shoulders and hands: who to see

Get a diagnosis first

A shoulder that stiffens over weeks, a finger that catches, numb fingers at night: see your doctor or a physiotherapist, not only a massage therapist.

What has the most support

Joint mobilisation plus exercise, from a physiotherapist or trained therapist. Largest effect in diabetes trials — low-quality evidence. Recovery tends to be slower with diabetes.

Where massage fits

Alongside, for muscle tension and comfort. Gentle range-of-motion work matters more than force.

If you’re offered a steroid injection

It can raise blood sugar for a while afterwards. Plan the days that follow with your diabetes team.

Sources: Alsubheen et al., Arch Phys Med Rehabil 2019 (8 trials) · Tang & Tan 2024 · Larkin et al. 2014. See also the Medications section on steroids.

Orthotherapists often see these first, because people book for “a stiff shoulder”. The most useful thing a therapist can say is “have you mentioned this to your diabetes team?”
