If your feet or hands have lost feeling

Neuropathy removes the one safety signal massage relies on: you saying “that’s too much”. So the therapist has to judge for you.

Broad pressure

On numb areas, ask for broad, full-hand pressure rather than deep point work or aggressive stretching.

No heat on numb skin

Hot stones, heat packs and hot towels can burn skin that can’t feel them. Diabetes Canada already says to test bath water by hand.

Look afterwards

The same foot check you already do daily, done again after the session: redness, blisters, broken skin.

A small signal of benefit

Two weeks of Thai foot massage improved balance and sensation tests in 60 people with type 2 and neuropathy. Small, short, type 2 — but encouraging.

Sources: Embil et al., Diabetes Canada foot care chapter 2018 · Chatchawan et al. 2015 · FQM 2007.

Tell the therapist where the numbness starts and stops. A map is more useful than the word “neuropathy”.
