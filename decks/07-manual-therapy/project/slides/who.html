Who does what in Québec

Four titles you’ll see on clinic doors. Only one of them belongs to a professional order today.

Massage therapist

Therapeutic massage. Not regulated in Québec: no professional order, and the title isn’t protected. Standards come from the association the therapist belongs to.

Orthotherapist

Massage plus movement therapy for muscle pain and joint stiffness — a specialisation after massage training, roughly 1,000 to 1,300 hours in all depending on the school. Also not regulated.

Kinesiologist

Exercise as treatment. Represented by a federation (FKQ). Québec announced in June 2026 that kinesiology will join an order; that isn’t in force yet.

Physiotherapist

Regulated by the OPPQ, with a public directory. The first call for an injury, a frozen shoulder, or anything that needs a diagnosis.

Sources: OQLF Grand dictionnaire terminologique · Mon Réseau Plus · RITMA · Gouvernement du Québec, 16 June 2026 · OPPQ. Ontario and BC regulate massage therapists; Québec does not.

In Québec a kinésithérapeute is an unregulated movement therapist, not the regulated French masseur-kinésithérapeute. People who have lived in France mix these up.
