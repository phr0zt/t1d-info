FOR PATIENTS, FAMILIES AND THE THERAPISTS WHO TREAT THEM

Massage, orthotherapy
and type 1 diabetes

What hands-on therapy can and can’t do for someone with type 1, the few things that change on the table, and what to tell the therapist before the first session.

Education, not medical advice. Insulin timing and site decisions belong with your diabetes team.

Open with the reassurance: most people with type 1 can book a massage like anyone else. This deck is about the handful of things that are different — mainly insulin, sensors and feet — not a list of reasons to stay away.
