For massage therapists and orthotherapists

Intake

Pump or injections; last dose and where

Glucose now, and its direction

Lows in the last day

Numb areas, wounds, dizziness standing up

On the table

A hand’s width clear around devices — no oil, no pressure

Skip recent injection sites; no heat there

Broad pressure on numb skin

Their fast sugar within their reach

Spot a low

Sweating, trembling, sudden restlessness, confusion, unusual quiet.

Stop. Sit them up slowly. Sugar first, questions after.

Unconscious or seizing: 911. Nothing by mouth.

Sources: FQM, « Masser une personne diabétique » · Diabète Québec · Dexcom G7 user guide · Diabetes Canada.

A sensor alarm during a session is not an interruption to apologise for. Give the client a moment to read it.
