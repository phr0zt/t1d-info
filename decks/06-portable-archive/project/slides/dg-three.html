The three you’ll actually meet

This deck covers a lot of substances. Be honest about the odds: for most people it’s these three, roughly in this order, and often all in the same night. So they get the most space.

01 · NICOTINE

Cigarettes and vapes

The slow one. It attacks the same blood vessels diabetes is already working on, and it makes your insulin do less work per unit.

02 · THC

Weed, edibles, carts

The forgetful one. Missed boluses, uncounted munchies, and symptoms you cannot tell apart from a hypo.

03 · ALCOHOL

Beer, coolers, shots

The delayed one. Your liver stops releasing glucose while it clears alcohol, so the hypo arrives while you’re asleep.

Stimulants, MDMA and psychedelics come later in the deck. Rarer — not less serious.

This slide sets expectations. Spending most of the time on the common three is a deliberate choice, not an oversight.
