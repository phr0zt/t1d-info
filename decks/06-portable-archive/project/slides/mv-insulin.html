Taking less insulin for planned exercise

The published starting points, for exercise within about 90 minutes of a meal dose. These are what your team will work from — bring them the plan, don’t apply them alone.

Meal dose before exercise 30 min 60 min
Easy aerobic−25%−50%
Moderate aerobic−50%−75%
Hard aerobic−75%—
Flat out or anaerobicNo reduction—

Cutting the meal dose by as much as 75% does not appear to raise ketones during exercise.

Background insulin

On a pump: reducing the basal rate 60 to 90 minutes beforehand works better than switching the pump off. If you do suspend, keep it short — Diabetes Canada says only for activity of 45 minutes or less, and never leave it off beyond about two hours.

On injections: cutting long-acting insulin is not routine advice, because it affects the whole day. It is an option for a tournament, a camp or a week of much heavier activity than usual.

Where you inject matters: not into a muscle you are about to work. Insulin leaves a working limb faster.

Sources: Riddell et al. 2017 Tables 2 and 3 · Diabetes Canada Recommendation 7 · ISPAD 2022

The last line is small and practical: quads before a run, abdomen before leg day. People rarely hear it and it changes results immediately.
