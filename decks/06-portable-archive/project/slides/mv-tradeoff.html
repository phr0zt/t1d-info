Exercise days have better numbers and more lows. Both are true.

In a study of 497 adults with type 1, days with exercise had 76% time in range against 70% on sedentary days, and less time running high.

The same days also had more time below range — and a low on 47% of exercise days against 40% of rest days.

Nobody should promise you that exercise is free. It costs you some lows.

The lows are manageable and the rest of this deck is how. The benefit is real and it is worth the management.
