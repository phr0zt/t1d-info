Nobody warns you. You find out from a number you can’t explain.

Almost every medicine you will ever take was studied mostly in people whose pancreas works. A handful of them move blood glucose, hard. The danger isn’t that they’re unsafe for you — it’s that the prescription arrives with no mention of it, and a week later you’re fighting numbers you assume are your fault.

The tone to set: this is not a list of drugs to refuse. It is a list of drugs to expect something from.
