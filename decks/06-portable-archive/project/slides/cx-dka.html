The complication that actually takes young lives is not on the last four slides

Of the years of life lost before age 50 in Scotland’s national type 1 data, the largest single share — around a quarter — came from diabetic ketoacidosis and diabetic coma. Not from eyes, kidneys or nerves.

DKA is an acute, fast, preventable emergency. Reported death rates range from under 1% to around 3%, and delay is what drives the higher end.

What sets it off

  • Illness and infection
  • A pump failing — no long-acting depot, so it moves fast
  • Insulin not taken: ran out, couldn’t afford it, too unwell to dose, or avoiding it

Which is why sick-day rules and ketone strips are not an optional extra. They are the part of diabetes education that saves the most life-years.

“Insulin omission” in the research covers all of those causes. It does not mean carelessness. Sources: Diabetes Canada CPG Ch.15 · Livingstone et al., Scotland.
