Three ways a medicine can get in the way

01

It pushes glucose up

Steroids are the big one, but they are not the only one. You need more insulin than usual, for as long as you’re on it.

02

It pushes glucose down — or hides the fall

The second half is worse than the first. A low you can still feel is a low you can still treat.

03

It leaves glucose alone but fools your sensor

Your body is fine. The number on the screen isn’t. You dose for a reading that was never real.

Category three surprises almost everyone, including some prescribers. It comes back later in the deck.
