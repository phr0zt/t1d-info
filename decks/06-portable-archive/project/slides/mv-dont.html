Eight things not to do

1

Don’t bench someone because of their diagnosis.

2

Don’t chase a pre-game high with insulin — it’s adrenaline and it settles.

3

Don’t stack corrections after a hard session. This one can kill.

4

Don’t trust a sensor number while glucose is dropping fast. Finger prick.

5

Don’t restart the session after a real low.

6

Don’t rely on a bedtime snack alone after afternoon sport.

7

Don’t train alone anywhere risky after a recent low.

8

Don’t forget to reconnect the pump.

Numbers two and three are the ones that produce genuine emergencies. If a coach remembers only two lines from the whole deck, make it those.
