Eyes

What happens

The tiny blood vessels in the retina get damaged. In later stages, fragile new vessels grow and can bleed. Fluid can also collect at the centre of vision, which is the commoner cause of sight loss.

How common

Diabetes Canada puts advanced eye disease at about 23% and central fluid at about 11% among people with type 1 — and those rates have been falling steadily for four decades.

Why screening matters most here

It is completely silent until it is advanced, and highly treatable when caught early. You cannot feel it coming and you will not notice it in a mirror.

Can it improve?

Early changes can and do regress — improvement was seen in 18% of participants in the largest long-term study. Advanced disease is treatable with laser and injections, and sight is usually preserved.

Clinically meaningful eye changes are rare in the first five years, which is why Canadian screening starts there. Source: Diabetes Canada CPG Ch.30.
