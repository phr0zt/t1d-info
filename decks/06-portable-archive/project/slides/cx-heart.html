Heart and blood vessels — the one that matters most

Eye and kidney disease get the attention. Cardiovascular disease is what actually accounts for most of the difference in life expectancy — in Scottish national data, ischaemic heart disease alone explained about a third of it.

Events are rare in young adulthood, but risk accumulates quietly from diagnosis onward. That is the whole argument for caring about cholesterol and blood pressure in your twenties, when nothing hurts.

And it is the most modifiable

This is the complication where things other than glucose do most of the work: blood pressure, cholesterol, not smoking, and staying active.

Most of that is a once-daily tablet and a pair of shoes, not another cognitive load on top of the twenty decisions a day diabetes already asks for.
