What the people around you actually need to know

Spot a low

  • Confused or slurring
  • Shaking, sweating, pale
  • Suddenly angry or odd
  • Drowsy, hard to rouse
  • Clumsy, or gone quiet

Fix it

If they can swallow, give sugar straight away — juice, glucose tablets, regular pop. Wait, then give them real food.

Sugar first, questions later. Always.

Call 911

If they can’t swallow, pass out, have a seizure, or can’t stop vomiting.

Say the words “they have type 1 diabetes”. Roll them onto their side. Nothing in the mouth.

Also worth knowing: where the fast sugar is, where the glucagon is, and that a delayed meal after someone has already dosed is a real problem, not a small one.

Ask people to show this slide to two others — a partner, a roommate, a sibling, a coworker. A plan nobody else knows about is not a plan.
