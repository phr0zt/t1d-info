Cigarettes

Diabetes slowly damages small blood vessels. Smoking does exactly the same thing. Put them together and you’re not doubling the risk — you’re bringing damage forward by decades.

Eyes

Retina damage — the leading cause of blindness in young adults.

Kidneys

Faster decline toward dialysis or a transplant.

Nerves and feet

Numbness, wounds that won’t heal, amputations.

Heart

Heart attack and stroke, far earlier than they should happen.

Nicotine also makes your body resist insulin, so the same dose does less work.

Teenagers discount things that happen in forty years. The insulin resistance line matters more here because it changes their numbers this month.
