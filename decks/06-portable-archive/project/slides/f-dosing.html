What people do about the late rise

These are the approaches your team may discuss with you. They are described here so the words are familiar when someone says them — not as something to try on your own.

On a pump

An extended or dual-wave bolus splits the dose: a common starting point is 60% right away and 40% stretched over about two and a half hours.

On injections

The usual dose at the meal, and a smaller second dose a few hours later. The evidence here is thinner, so it gets worked out person by person.

A starting point

ISPAD suggests around 20% extra insulin for a high-fat, high-protein meal, then adjusting from what actually happens.

Why not to improvise

A second dose given on a wrong guess is a night-time hypo. This is the highest-risk adjustment in the whole deck.

Sources: ISPAD 2022 · Cambridge University Hospitals NHS fat and protein bolusing guidance. Bring the pattern to your team — not the fix.

Say plainly that the right move here is showing your team three nights of data, not experimenting alone.
