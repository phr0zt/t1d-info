When not to train today

Blood ketones 1.5 or above

Exercise is off, full stop. At 3.0 or above this is an emergency department, not a rest day.

Ketones 0.6 to 1.4

Postpone. Find out why, and take a correction dose as your plan sets out before considering anything.

A severe low in the last 24 hours

One needing someone else’s help. Your hormonal defences are blunted, so the next one comes easier and harder.

Illness, infection or injury

Vigorous exercise is out. Both push glucose up and change what your insulin does.

And a myth worth killing: “don’t exercise above 14” is wrong as stated. Every guideline conditions this on ketones, not on the glucose number by itself.

Mild high glucose does not measurably hurt exercise capacity. It is insulin deficiency with ketones that makes exercise dangerous, and those are different things.
