You’ve heard the drug talk. This is the part they leave out.

Most people have a pancreas quietly fixing their blood sugar in the background, whatever they do to themselves. You don’t. Every drug — legal or not — lands on a body with no backup system, and the one person running that system is you, at the exact moment you’re least able to.

The point of this slide is respect, not fear. Their risk is genuinely different from their friends' risk, and that is a fact rather than a scare tactic.
