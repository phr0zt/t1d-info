What type 1 actually is

An autoimmune condition

The immune system destroyed the cells in the pancreas that make insulin. It is the same category of thing as coeliac disease or thyroid disease — which is why those often turn up alongside it.

Nobody caused it

Not sugar. Not diet. Not parenting. Not anything anyone did or failed to do. This is worth saying to every relative who will be told about the diagnosis.

It is not type 2

Different cause, different treatment, different risks. Almost everything the public believes about diabetes is about type 2, and most of it does not transfer.

Insulin is not optional

There is no diet, supplement or amount of exercise that replaces it. Anyone selling otherwise is selling something dangerous.

Roughly three in four people with type 1 report experiencing stigma — most often being seen as having a character flaw. Nearly all of that comes from being confused with type 2.

The stigma figure explains a lot of what people with type 1 experience socially, and it tends to land hard with families who have unknowingly contributed to it.
