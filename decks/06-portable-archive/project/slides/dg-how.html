Nearly every drug hurts you the same three ways

01

You forget your insulin

Time stops meaning anything. You skip a dose, or you can’t remember if you took one and double up. Both are dangerous.

02

Your eating goes sideways

Some drugs make you eat everything in the house. Others switch your appetite off completely. Your blood sugar follows.

03

You can’t feel a hypo coming

Shaky, sweaty, confused, heart pounding. That’s a hypo. It’s also just being high. You lose the ability to tell them apart.

Number three is the one that surprises people. The warning system they have been trained to trust for years stops being readable.
