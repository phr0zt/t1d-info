Nerves

Longest nerves first

Feet, then legs, then hands. Either burning and shooting pain, or — more dangerously — numbness, because losing protective sensation is what makes foot injuries possible.

Read the number carefully

Diabetes Canada says nerve changes are detectable on examination within 10 years in 40 to 50% of people with type 1. Detectable is a much wider category than painful or troubling. Symptoms are uncommon in the first five years.

Nerves that run the body

The same process can affect nerves controlling the heart rate, the bladder and the stomach. Slow stomach emptying affects roughly 5% over ten years, usually after other complications appear.

An honest point

Nerve damage is prevented far better than it is reversed, and slow stomach emptying in particular does not reliably improve even when glucose is in range afterwards. Anyone told that is their fault has been told something untrue.

Nerve pain itself is treatable — several medications routinely reduce it by a third to a half. Sources: Diabetes Canada CPG Ch.31 · Diabetes Spectrum.

The last card exists because gastroparesis is the complication most often blamed on the person who has it, and the evidence does not support that blame.
