Your pharmacist is the most underused person in your care

They are the only person who sees every prescription from every doctor you have — including the ones your endocrinologist never hears about, and the walk-in clinic that prescribed the prednisone.

No appointment, no wait, no fee. And they will happily go through the whole list with you.

Three habits

Use one pharmacy for everything, so the interaction check actually has all the pieces.

Say “I have type 1 diabetes” every single time, even when you assume it’s on file.

Book a medication review once a year. In most provinces it is covered.

For a newly diagnosed person, naming the pharmacist as part of the care team is genuinely new information.
