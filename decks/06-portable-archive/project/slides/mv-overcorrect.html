The most dangerous moment is the high after a hard session.

You finish a tough workout, the number is up, and it feels wrong. So you correct. Then it’s still up, so you correct again. Meanwhile your muscles are about to start pulling glucose out for the next several hours, and the insulin is stacking.

The consensus statement says it without softening: over-correcting with insulin after exercise can cause severe night-time hypoglycemia and lead to death.

Correct conservatively, or wait. This is a slide for coaches and parents as much as for patients.

There is a live research disagreement about the right correction size after interval training. For a general audience, stay with the conservative guideline position and refer the question to their team.
