Steroids deserve their own slide

A short course of prednisone for a chest infection, or one cortisone shot in a knee, can double what you need for days. Most people are never told.

Prednisone, once each morning

The rise begins around four hours after the dose and can run twelve. The classic shape is a normal morning, a high afternoon and evening, and a better dawn.

Dexamethasone

Works for twelve to thirty-six hours, so it covers the night too. There is no quiet window to hide in.

The injection counts

A joint, spine or skin injection is a steroid dose. People don’t report it because it didn’t come in a bottle.

The taper is the dangerous end

As the dose comes down, your insulin needs fall with it. Stay on the raised doses and the lows arrive instead.

Call your team when a steroid is prescribed — not three high days later. This is a planned adjustment, and they do it all the time.

Ask people to say "I have type 1 diabetes" at the moment any steroid is offered, including by a physio or a surgeon.
