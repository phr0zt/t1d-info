Medicines that push blood sugar up

Corticosteroids

Prednisone, dexamethasone, cortisone injections. By far the largest effect — own slide next.

Some antipsychotics

Olanzapine and quetiapine especially. They reduce how well insulin works.

Thiazide diuretics

Hydrochlorothiazide and relatives, common for blood pressure.

Decongestants

Pseudoephedrine, sold over the counter in cold and sinus products.

Beta blockers

Up a little — and they hide lows, which matters more. Own slide shortly.

Hormonal contraceptives

Can shift glucose handling. Worth watching the first few cycles.

Some HIV medicines

Protease inhibitors in particular are linked to higher glucose.

Transplant medicines

Tacrolimus and cyclosporine raise glucose and are usually non-negotiable.

None of these is a reason to refuse a medicine you need. They are reasons to expect the change, and to ask what to do about it first.
