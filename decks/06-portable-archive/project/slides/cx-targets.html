Read the first two together. Guideline-level success includes roughly seven hours a day outside range and about an hour low. If every high reading feels like a failure, the target itself says otherwise.

Time-in-range targets come from the 2019 international consensus and are for most non-pregnant adults; older adults, children and pregnancy have their own. Source: Battelino et al., Diabetes Care 2019 · Diabetes Canada CPG Ch.8.

The closing line is the point of the slide. Perfectionism drives burnout, and the guideline itself is the best argument against it.
