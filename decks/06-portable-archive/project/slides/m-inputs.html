Three levers you pull every day

01

Food

Pushes glucose up. Carbohydrate does nearly all of it, fast. Fat and protein arrive late and quietly.

02

Insulin

Pushes glucose down. How much matters, and when matters nearly as much.

03

Movement

Pushes it either way. Steady effort lowers it; hard, short effort raises it. And the effect lasts long after you stop.

Everything after these three — medication, alcohol, illness, stress, heat, hormones, sleep — is interference on top. Which is why two identical days can give two different numbers, and neither is anybody’s fault.

The closing line pre-empts the most common source of self-blame: doing the same thing twice and getting different results.
