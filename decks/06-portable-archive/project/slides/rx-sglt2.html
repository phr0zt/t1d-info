The one where DKA arrives with a normal reading

SGLT2 inhibitors

Empagliflozin (Jardiance), dapagliflozin (Forxiga), canagliflozin (Invokana).

Type 2 diabetes drugs, also prescribed for heart failure and kidney disease — so people with type 1 do end up on them.

They work by dumping glucose out through your urine. In type 1 that creates a specific trap: you can go into full diabetic ketoacidosis while your glucose meter reads normal. It is called euglycemic DKA.

The alarm you have relied on since diagnosis — a high number — never sounds. Health Canada and the regulators have flagged this risk specifically.

If you are on one: ketone strips, not just glucose. Any vomiting, stomach pain or unusual breathlessness gets a ketone check regardless of the number.

Use in type 1 is off-label in most places and is a decision to make with a specialist, before starting — never a pill to try because it helped someone else.

If someone in the room is already taking one and did not know this, that is the most valuable thirty seconds of the whole deck.
