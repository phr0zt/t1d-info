Five things that aren’t true

“They can’t take that.”

Almost nothing is off-limits. It is about expecting the effect and planning around it.

“The insulin dose is fixed.”

It moves with illness, steroids, activity and hormones. Changing it is the management, not a failure of it.

“Vaccines are risky with diabetes.”

Backwards. Flu and similar illnesses are what drive people into DKA, which is why annual vaccination is recommended.

“Sugar-free means free.”

Sugar alcohols still carry carbohydrate, and in quantity they upset stomachs. Read the whole panel.

“The sensor says it’s fine.”

Not on hydroxyurea, not above maximum acetaminophen doses, and not when the number and the person disagree.

The vaccine one is worth stating plainly. It circulates widely and it gets people hospitalised.
