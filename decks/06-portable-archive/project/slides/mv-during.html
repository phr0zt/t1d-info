While you’re moving

Check often

Every 30 minutes on finger pricks, every 20 on a sensor. A bench, a water break or a set rest is the moment to look.

Your sensor runs late

Sensors read fluid under the skin, not blood, and lag by roughly 12 to 24 minutes. When glucose is dropping fast, the screen still shows the number from ten minutes ago — too high.

Trust the symptoms

If you feel low and the sensor disagrees, finger prick. That habit is worth more during sport than at any other time.

Below 3.0, you’re done

Treat it, and do not restart the session. Going again after a real low is how one bad afternoon turns into a dangerous one.

Sources: EASD/ISPAD CGM position statement · ISPAD 2022. Carry fast glucose on your body, not in a bag on the sideline.

Compression lows are worth mentioning to cyclists and anyone in tight kit: pressure on a sensor gives falsely low readings.
