Glucose is the biggest lever. It is one of five.

Swedish registry work models outcomes by how many of five risk factors are on target, and risk rises step by step for each one that is not. Four of the five are not a 24-hour cognitive load.

Glucose

A1C at or under 7.0%, time in range over 70%. The one that takes constant attention.

Blood pressure

Under 130 over 80. Usually one tablet, checked at every visit.

Cholesterol

LDL under 2.0, or halved. A statin is indicated from 40, or from 30 with 15 years’ duration.

Kidney protection

An ACE inhibitor or ARB where indicated — the strongest kidney intervention there is, independent of glucose.

Not smoking

And moving regularly. Both sit in the same model as the other four.

The honest limit: even with all five on target, kidney risk in type 1 stays above the general population. Risk control roughly halves the excess rather than erasing it. Source: Rawshani et al., Diabetes Care 2022.

This slide is genuinely good news and is chronically undersold. Four of the five levers are handled at an appointment, not at every meal.
