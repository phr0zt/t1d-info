What the people around you need to know

Spot it

  • Confused or slurring
  • Shaking, sweating, pale
  • Suddenly angry or odd
  • Drowsy, hard to rouse

Fix it

If they can swallow, give sugar straight away — juice, jellybeans, regular (not diet) pop.

Wait, then give them real food. Sugar first, questions later.

Call 911

If they can’t swallow, pass out, have a seizure, or can’t stop vomiting.

Say the words “they have type 1 diabetes”. Roll them onto their side. Nothing in the mouth.

Ambulances aren’t police. Nobody gets charged for calling one.

Suggest they actually show this slide to two people — a partner, a roommate, a sibling, whoever is usually there. A plan nobody else knows about is not a plan.
