This isn’t a bad comedown. It’s DKA.

  • Stomach pain
  • Throwing up, and it won’t stop
  • Breath smells sweet or like nail polish
  • Breathing fast and deep
  • Very thirsty, peeing constantly
  • Drowsy, hard to keep awake

Check your ketones. Tell someone. Get to a hospital or call 911.

Say it out loud: nobody has ever been in trouble for arriving at an emergency department with ketones. The delay is what does the damage.
