MDMA has its own problem: water

MDMA heats you up, keeps you moving, and stops your body clearing water properly. There are two ways to get this wrong, and they point in opposite directions.

Too little water

Dehydration pushes your BGLs up and concentrates ketones. It’s a direct road to DKA, and high blood sugar dehydrates you further.

Too much water

Skolling bottle after bottle dilutes the salt in your blood to dangerous levels. People have died this way, without a single extra pill.

Roughly right

About half a litre an hour if you’re dancing, less if you’re sitting. Sip it. Get out of the heat. Take breaks.

MDMA also causes irregular heart rhythms — a real concern if your diabetes has been hard to control.

Stress that the water advice is a rate, not a target. Steady sipping, not catching up in one go.
