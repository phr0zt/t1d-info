Fixing a hypo: the 15 rule

1

Stop what you’re doing and sit down.

2

6–7 jellybeans, a small juice, or half a can of regular (not diet) pop.

3

Wait 15 minutes. Check again.

4

Still low? Do it again.

5

Then slow carbs — sandwich, banana, crackers.

Diet drinks do nothing. Chocolate is too slow — the fat holds the sugar back.

Worth rehearsing. In a real hypo they will not be reading a slide, so the sequence has to already be in their head.
