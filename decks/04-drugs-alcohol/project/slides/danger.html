Two ways it goes wrong

Too low — a hypo

Comes on in minutes. Caused by stimulants, alcohol, dancing all night, not eating.

Left alone it goes to seizures and unconsciousness. This is the fast one.

Too high — DKA

Builds over hours. Missed insulin, ketones pile up, your blood turns acidic.

It does not fix itself and it lands people in intensive care. This is the slow one.

Fast versus slow is the useful mental model. A hypo is minutes and needs sugar now. DKA is hours and needs a hospital.
