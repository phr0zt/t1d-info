If you’re going to anyway, do these seven things

1

Never skip your insulin. Not once, not “just tonight”.

2

Never use alone. If you have to, NORS will stay on the line: 1-888-688-6677.

3

One sober person there knows you have type 1 and knows where your hypo food is.

4

Wear your medical ID. Paramedics look for it first.

5

Fast sugar in your pocket, not in a bag across the room.

6

Phone alarms to check your BGL. You will not remember on your own.

7

Eat carbs before, during and after — and know what you took.

Frame this as harm reduction, not permission. The alternative to saying it is them doing all of it with none of it.
