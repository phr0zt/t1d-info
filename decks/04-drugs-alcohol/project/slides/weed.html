Weed

The one people assume is fine — and after alcohol and nicotine, the one most of us actually encounter. The problem isn’t that cannabis attacks your blood sugar directly. It’s that it takes away the three things diabetes needs from you.

Memory

You genuinely cannot remember whether you bolused. So you skip it, or you take it twice. One sends you toward ketones, the other toward the floor.

The munchies

Half a loaf of bread and most of a box of cereal, uncounted and unbolused. Your BGLs go up and stay up for hours.

Time

“I’ll check in a minute” becomes four hours. Nothing feels urgent, which is precisely the problem.

This is the slide that usually changes minds, because it avoids the moral argument entirely and sticks to mechanism.
