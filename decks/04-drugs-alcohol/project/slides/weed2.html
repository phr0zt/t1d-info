Greening out, or something serious?

Too much weed — especially an edible — leaves you pale, sweaty, dizzy and throwing up.

So does a hypo.

So does DKA.

You cannot tell them apart by how you feel. There is no version of you that can.

So check. Every time.

Finger prick or sensor for your BGL. If you’re high and vomiting, check ketones too.

Thirty seconds of effort rules out the thing that could kill you.

Edibles take 1–3 hours to hit and last far longer — the dose you took an hour ago is still on its way.

The edible timing note prevents the classic mistake of redosing because nothing seemed to happen.
