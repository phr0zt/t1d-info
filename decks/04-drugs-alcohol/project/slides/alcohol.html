Alcohol — the one that gets people at 3am

Your liver has two jobs: clear alcohol out, and release glucose when you’re running low. It will not do the second one while it’s busy with the first.

So the hypo doesn’t arrive while you’re drinking. It arrives hours later, in bed, asleep.

And being drunk and being hypo look identical from the outside. That’s the whole danger.

If you drink

  • Eat carbs while you drink, not instead of dinner
  • Never skip your long-acting insulin
  • Eat something before bed
  • Set an alarm and actually check overnight
  • Tell whoever you’re staying with

Sugary mixers spike you first, then the alcohol drags you down later — up, then hard down.

Alcohol is statistically the one they will meet first, so give it the same weight as the illegal drugs.
