Birthday cake, loot bags and kids’ parties

Going to parties matters for a child’s friendships and independence. The answer is never to stay home — it’s to plan a bit, and accept that it won’t be perfect.

Before

Ask the host what food there is and roughly when. Suggest the running-around games come after eating rather than before.

During

A plate of food is easier to dose than two hours of grazing. Activity lowers glucose, so a party is not an ordinary afternoon.

The cake, and the loot bag

Both are yes. The sweets in the bag double as hypo treatment later — there is no reason for a child to be the only one without one.

Afterwards, don’t withhold food

If they’re running high that evening, insulin fixes that — skipping dinner doesn’t. Serve sensible carbs, check before bed, and snack if needed to prevent an overnight low.

Source: Breakthrough T1D party guidance. Withholding food to correct a high is ineffective and a step toward disordered eating.

The last card is counterintuitive and worth saying twice. The instinct to compensate by restricting is strong and it does real harm.
