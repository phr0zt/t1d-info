There is no diabetic diet.

No forbidden list. No special aisle. People with type 1 can eat what everyone else eats — birthday cake included — given the right insulin at roughly the right time.

What changes isn’t what goes on the plate. It’s that somebody now has to estimate what’s in it, and do the job a pancreas used to do silently, three or four times a day, forever.

Kill this myth first. Everything else in the deck is easier to hear once the audience stops expecting a list of banned foods.
