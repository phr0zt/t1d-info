Reading a Canadian Nutrition Facts table

One Canadian rule makes everything else simple: “Carbohydrate” is already the total. Sugars, starch, fibre and sugar alcohols are all inside that number.

Never add the sugars line

Sugars are already counted inside Carbohydrate. Adding them on top is the most common label mistake, and it doubles the dose.

Subtract fibre in full

It’s inside the total and your body doesn’t absorb it, so it comes straight back off.

Sugar alcohols: about half

Maltitol, sorbitol, xylitol, erythritol. Partly absorbed, unpredictably. Diabète Québec suggests subtracting half — then watching what your own body does.

Start at the serving size

Every number in the table is per serving, not per package. The serving is often half of what anyone actually eats.

Aspartame, stevia and sucralose carry no carbohydrate and never enter the count. Sources: Canadian Food Inspection Agency · Diabète Québec · Diabetes Canada.

Guidance on sugar alcohols is genuinely split between subtracting all of them and half. Half is the conservative choice and matches the Quebec source.
