Eating when you’re too sick to eat

The insulin still goes in

Illness pushes blood sugar up even with no food. Skipping insulin because you haven’t eaten is how people end up in DKA.

Carbs in liquid form

If solid food won’t stay down, carbohydrate comes as regular soft drink, juice, sports drink, ice pops or sweetened tea — sipped steadily, not gulped.

Salt and water too

Broth, bouillon or an oral rehydration solution. Dehydration concentrates ketones and makes everything worse faster.

Check ketones

Whatever the glucose reads. Moderate or large ketones, or vomiting that won’t stop, means calling for help rather than managing at home.

Have a written sick-day plan from your team before you need it. Sick judgement is bad judgement, and the person reading it may be a parent or partner.
