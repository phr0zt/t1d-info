Going gluten-free makes blood sugar harder, not easier

This surprises everyone. If celiac is confirmed, a gluten-free diet is the treatment and is not optional — but expect the numbers to get bumpier for a while, for two separate reasons.

Gluten-free products carry less fibre

So their carbohydrate arrives faster and peaks higher. Gluten-free bread is not a like-for-like swap for the bread it replaced.

Your gut starts absorbing properly again

As it heals, you take up more of what you eat than you used to. Some people need more insulin after starting, not less.

What helps

Higher-fibre and lower-GI gluten-free choices, and getting the insulin in ahead of the meal. Certified gluten-free oats are usually fine in moderation.

And for everyone else

Without confirmed celiac, going gluten-free does nothing for type 1. Don’t put someone on it as a favour.

Sources: ISPAD 2022 · Breakthrough T1D. Watch numbers closely during the first weeks of the change.

The last card matters for families. Well-meaning relatives put people on elimination diets that help nothing and complicate dosing.
