Five things people still say

“They can’t have sugar.”

They can, with insulin to cover it. Sugar is carbohydrate like any other — it just arrives faster.

“Fruit is too sugary.”

Fruit is counted, not avoided. Whole fruit brings fibre and arrives more slowly than the juice made from it.

“I bought the diabetic ones.”

Diabetic-branded foods are a marketing category, not a medical one. Usually pricier, often no easier to dose.

“No sugar added, so it’s fine.”

No sugar added is not carb-free. Sugar alcohols still count, and in quantity they upset stomachs.

“A high number means they cheated.”

Numbers move with illness, stress, sleep, hormones, heat and yesterday’s pizza. A reading is information, not a verdict on someone’s character.

The last card is the one to dwell on. It reframes every number in the deck as data rather than as a moral score.
