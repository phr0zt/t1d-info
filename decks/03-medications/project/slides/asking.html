Four questions, before anything new goes in

1

Will this move my blood sugar, and in which direction?

2

When — straight away, hours later, or only for the first week?

3

Does it interact with insulin, or hide the signs of a low?

4

Do I hold this on a sick day — and does it change my insulin plan?

Ask the same four about vitamins, herbal products and anything bought online. “Natural” does not mean it does nothing — it usually means nobody measured what it does.

Suggest putting these four in a phone note. At the counter, under pressure, nobody remembers them unprompted.
